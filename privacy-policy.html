核心承诺：读图 . ReadImg 应用完全在您的设备本地运行，不上传您的图片或识别文字到我们的服务器。您的隐私安全是我们的首要考虑。

1. 信息收集

读图 . ReadImg 应用不会把您的图片或识别文字上传到我们的服务器。具体而言：

  • 图片数据：您选择或拍摄的图片仅在设备本地进行处理，不会上传到任何服务器
  • 识别结果：文字识别完全在设备本地完成，识别结果不会发送到外部
  • 使用数据：应用内可能会记录有限的本地设置和匿名产品事件，用于功能控制、购买状态和基础产品分析
  • 设备信息：我们不会读取您的照片内容用于广告画像或出售给第三方

2. 权限使用说明

为了提供核心功能，应用需要以下权限：

  • 相机权限：用于拍摄图片进行文字识别。拍摄的照片仅用于识别，不会被保存或上传
  • 照片库权限：用于从相册选择图片进行文字识别。我们只读取您选择的图片，不会访问其他照片

您可以随时在系统设置中撤销这些权限。

3. 数据存储

读图 . ReadImg 应用不会把您的图片上传到服务器。应用可能在设备本地保存以下数据，以提供连续阅读和相册内搜索等功能：

  • 您的语音设置偏好（如语速、音调、音量）
  • 您设置的常用相册列表（使用系统 Keychain 安全存储）
  • 收藏图片列表
  • 阅读进度与引导完成状态
  • 当您启用“相册内搜索”时，应用可能为当前相册在本地建立 OCR 文字索引缓存，以加快后续搜索

这些数据默认仅保存在您的设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器。相册内搜索缓存最多保留最近 10 个相册；如果您在设置中关闭该功能，相关本地缓存会被清除。

4. 第三方服务

读图 . ReadImg 应用不投放广告，也不会出售您的数据。应用可能使用有限的第三方服务来支持基础功能，例如应用内购买和匿名产品分析，但不会上传您的图片内容或 OCR 索引文本。

5. 数据安全

由于所有处理都在设备本地完成，您的数据享有 iOS 系统提供的安全保护。我们不传输任何数据，因此不存在数据传输过程中的安全风险。

6. 儿童隐私

读图 . ReadImg 应用适合所有年龄段使用。由于我们不收集任何个人信息，因此不存在儿童隐私相关的问题。

7. 隐私政策更新

如果我们对隐私政策进行重大更改，将通过应用更新说明通知您。建议您定期查看本页面以了解最新的隐私保护措施。

Core Commitment: ReadImg runs primarily on your device locally and does not upload your images or recognized text to our servers. Your privacy is a top priority.

1. Information Collection

ReadImg does not upload your images or recognized text to our servers. Specifically:

  • Image Data: Images you select or capture are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server
  • Recognition Results: Text recognition is performed entirely on your device; results are never sent externally
  • Usage Data: The app may store limited local settings and anonymous product events for feature control, purchase status, and basic product analytics
  • Device Information: We do not use your photo content for advertising profiles or sell it to third parties

2. Permissions

To provide core functionality, the app requires the following permissions:

  • Camera Permission: Used to capture photos for text recognition. Photos are only used for recognition and are not saved or uploaded
  • Photo Library Permission: Used to select images for text recognition. We only access images you explicitly select

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

3. Data Storage

ReadImg does not upload your images to our servers. The app may store the following data locally on your device to support continuous reading and in-album search:

  • Your voice settings preferences (speech rate, pitch, volume)
  • Your favorite albums list (securely stored using system Keychain)
  • Your saved images list
  • Reading progress and onboarding completion status
  • When you enable in-album search, a local OCR text index cache for the current album to speed up later searches

This data is stored locally on your device by default and is not uploaded to our servers. In-album search keeps caches for up to the 10 most recent albums. If you turn this feature off in settings, related local caches are cleared.

4. Third-Party Services

ReadImg does not serve ads and does not sell your data. The app may use limited third-party services for core functionality, such as in-app purchases and anonymous product analytics, but it does not upload your image content or OCR index text.

5. Data Security

Since all processing is done locally on your device, your data is protected by iOS system security. We do not transmit any data, so there are no security risks during data transmission.

6. Children's Privacy

ReadImg is suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, there are no children's privacy concerns.

7. Privacy Policy Updates

If we make significant changes to this privacy policy, we will notify you through app update notes. We recommend checking this page periodically for the latest privacy practices.
